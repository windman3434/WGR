We keep gravel racing simple

Each event combines scenic, not-too-demanding courses with the excitement of lining up at a start — all located close to Warsaw, never more than an hour away. No matter your speed, you’ll share the experience, celebrate at the finish, and maybe discover a new love for the gravel community along the way — and if you’re fast, there’s plenty of room to truly race. You choose if you take part with your team/company or alone. One event or the whole season.
