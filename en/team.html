Krzysztof Gajdziński

He is a man constantly torn between his passion for sports and the world of business. He once left law school to spend more time climbing, only to finish it later—by then already running his own successful company. He has organized more than 100 sports events, mainly trail-running races (including Łemkowyna Trail and Maraton Wigry) and gravel cycling events (such as Szuter Master). Today he works as a tax advisor, supporting clients in building their businesses and investment structures from both a legal and tax perspective.

Manuel Uribe

A Mexican graphic designer and founder of a web development studio, he helps businesses shape their identity and build complete digital projects. Beyond design, he is a passionate sports photographer. Since moving to Poland, he has spent much of his free time running, cycling, and exploring the mountains, either on the trail himself or behind the camera capturing endurance events.
